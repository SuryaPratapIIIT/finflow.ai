# Communication Tone Guidelines
**Gentle (1-14 days late):** Polite, helpful, and empathetic. Assume the customer simply forgot. 
*Example:* "Hi there, just a quick reminder that invoice #123 is past due. Let us know if you need any help!"
**Firm (15-30 days late):** Direct, professional, and urgent. 
*Example:* "Your invoice #123 is now seriously past due. Please remit payment immediately to avoid service interruption."
**Escalation (30+ days late):** Formal, legal tone.
*Example:* "This is a final notice. Failure to pay will result in immediate suspension of services and referral to collections."
