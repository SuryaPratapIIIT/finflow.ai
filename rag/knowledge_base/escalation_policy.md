# Collections Escalation Policy
- **1-14 Days Overdue:** Gentle email reminders sent every 3 days. Focus on customer service and resolving payment hurdles.
- **15-30 Days Overdue:** Firm email reminders and a phone call from the account manager. Services may be restricted.
- **31-60 Days Overdue:** Account is suspended. Final warning letters are issued. 
- **60+ Days Overdue:** Account is referred to an external legal collections agency.
